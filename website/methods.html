Analytical design

Methods built to refuse the wrong data.

The default pipeline is empirical. Provenance gates exclude synthetic and unclassified rows; longitudinal tests return not_testable when no repeated observed series exists.

02 · Analytical modules

Four forms of analysis, four scopes of inference

Open each module to inspect its input, computation, output, and non-claim.

M1Cross-platform entity matchingObserved archive · descriptive
Observed

Artist and title strings are Unicode-normalized, ASCII-folded, lowercased, and stripped of non-alphanumeric characters. Release year completes the key. Duplicate keys and fuzzy matches are excluded.

  • AOTY scores are rescaled from 0–100 to 0–5.
  • Pearson and Spearman statistics summarize agreement.
  • Difference distributions quantify calibration offsets.
  • Archive dates and selection differences prevent longitudinal inference.
M2Attention and genre structureObserved archive · within-sample
Observed

Rating-count Gini coefficients and top-share measures characterize concentration within each selected file. Genre strings are split and the twelve shared genres with strongest minimum coverage are retained.

  • Cell values remain in original units.
  • Heatmap colour is standardized within each metric.
  • High-rating-selected AOTY and popularity-selected RYM files are not direct market-size comparators.
M3Structural-change diagnosticsEmpirical design · synthetic method check
Synthetic benchmark

November 2022 is a prespecified candidate date. The implementation includes Welch pre/post comparison, a regression Chow test, detrended CUSUM with permutation-bootstrap inference, and dynamic-programming least-squares segmentation with BIC selection. R03–R04

  • The multiple-break method implements the least-squares segmentation core associated with Bai–Perron.
  • It does not implement supF/UDmax tests, robust covariance corrections, or breakpoint confidence intervals.
  • Default empirical mode returns not_testable because the archives contain no repeated rating timestamps.
M4Controlled text comparisonHybrid corpus · pipeline inspection
Controlled

Fifteen source-diverse published critic excerpts are compared with fifteen fixed, manually authored assistant-style controls. Five-fold stratified out-of-fold TF–IDF classification avoids train/test leakage.

  • The controls are not outputs from a documented generative model.
  • There is no platform-user holdout or external validation.
  • Accuracy and AUC describe separation between two deliberately contrasted groups—not AI authorship detection.
M5Trust, policy, and platform scenariosAssumption-driven · conditional
Scenario

Trust curves, user-group differences, intervention comparisons, and platform positions are deterministic outputs under selected parameters or analyst-coded ordinal scores.

  • Sensitivity figures show how outputs move as assumptions change.
  • Values do not estimate current AI penetration, user trust, policy effects, or organizational readiness.
  • The modules clarify mechanisms and measurement targets for later empirical work.

The decisive design is longitudinal.

A causal timing claim needs repeated, provenance-rich observations.

D1
Repeat the same albums or users

Preserve rating counts, review counts, score distributions, and contribution activity across dated snapshots.

D2
Record rule and interface changes

Ranking, moderation, export, visibility, and chart-weight changes can independently shift outcomes.

D3
Prespecify exclusions and alternatives

Catalog mix, cohort turnover, seasonality, platform growth, and policy changes must be distinguished from an AI-related shock.

D4
Measure contributor-level mechanisms

Review depth, taxonomy work, retention, trust, and appeals can change before aggregate traffic does.
